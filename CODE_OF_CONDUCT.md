# Code of conduct

Codr follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **admin@sasateguh.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential; the maintainer may remove comments, issues or pull requests and block people who break this code.
